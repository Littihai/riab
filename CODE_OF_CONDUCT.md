# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their background, language or experience. Thai and English are both welcome in issues.
- Critique the work, not the person. Assume good intent, and say what would help.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in any form.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (mark it as a conduct report) or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
